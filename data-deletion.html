Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: April 2025

Introduction

YourFluence respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Data Deletion Policy explains how we handle your personal information and data obtained through Instagram's Meta API, in compliance with Meta's Platform Policies. We ensure that all data is deleted when no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, and in compliance with Meta’s requirements on data retention and deletion.

Data Retention Period

We retain user data only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the legitimate business purposes for which it was collected, in line with Meta's data retention guidelines. These purposes include:

  • Providing services related to marketing campaign tracking and other functionalities of the YourFluence platform.
  • Maintaining communication with users regarding campaigns and marketing performance.
  • Complying with legal obligations or as required by law.

If you revoke access or uninstall the app, we will delete your personal data as soon as it is no longer required for the purposes outlined above, and within 30 days of access being revoked or the app being uninstalled. Data will also be deleted within 30 days after the completion of the relevant campaign or marketing activity.

Automatic Data Deletion

We automatically delete user data in the following scenarios:

  • Revocation of Data Access: If a user revokes access or uninstalls the YourFluence app, we will delete all personal data associated with that user within 30 days of such action.
  • Campaign Completion: Once a marketing campaign is completed, the data used for that campaign is no longer needed. Any data related to the campaign will be deleted within 30 days after campaign completion.
  • Expiration of Data Access: If the access granted to us via the Instagram API (Meta API) expires or is otherwise terminated, all data linked to that access will be deleted within 30 days.
  • End of Business Purpose: If we determine that the data is no longer necessary for the business purpose for which it was collected (e.g., user inactivity), it will be deleted within 30 days of this determination.

User-Initiated Data Deletion

If you wish to manually request the deletion of your data, you can do so at any time by following these steps:

  • Contact via Email: Send an email to info@yourfluence.nl with the subject line: Data Deletion Request. Please include your name, the email address associated with your account, and your Instagram username (if applicable).
  • In-App Request: You may submit a request directly through the support feature within the app, specifying your data deletion request.

Upon receiving your request, we will verify your identity and ensure that your data is deleted within 30 days. We will notify you once your request has been processed and your data is removed from our system.

Deletion Confirmation

Once your data is deleted, we will confirm via email or in-app notification that the deletion process has been completed. You will no longer have access to any of the deleted data, and it will not be recoverable.

Data Deletion After Uninstallation of the App

If you uninstall the YourFluence app, your data will be deleted within 30 days of uninstallation. If you choose to reinstall the app, we may require you to re-authorize access to your Instagram account, but your previous data will not be retained unless explicitly required for reactivation of services.

How We Handle Data from Meta APIs

As part of our integration with Instagram (Meta), we receive certain data from users' Instagram accounts through the Meta API. This data includes information such as:

  • Publicly available information from Instagram accounts, such as profile information and content shared by users.
  • Engagement data related to Instagram posts or stories.

We store this data temporarily to facilitate your use of the YourFluence platform and marketing campaigns. Once the data is no longer needed for the business purpose it was collected, it is deleted in accordance with the timelines mentioned above.

How Data is Secured

We implement robust security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or corruption. However, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We take all reasonable steps to ensure the safety of your data.

Data Retention Exceptions

In some cases, we may retain data for longer periods due to legal, regulatory, or other compliance requirements. If applicable, we will inform you of such retention periods, and such data will be securely archived and protected.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Data Deletion Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at info@yourfluence.nl. We are committed to addressing your concerns and ensuring your privacy is respected.
